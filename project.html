PROJECT 001 / IN DEVELOPMENT

LIFE
ONLINE.

A large-scale open-world life simulation concept inspired by the real world — its places, movement, systems and everyday experiences.

CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT
EARTH / CONCEPT STUDY
01 / THE IDEA

A world that feels
connected.

LIFE ONLINE aims to bring together exploration, transportation, places to visit, activities and systems of everyday life in one connected experience. The project takes inspiration from the scale of Earth and the variety of ways people move through and interact with the world.

This is a project vision, not a list of completed or currently playable features.

02 / DESIGN PILLARS
01

Explore

Discover environments inspired by real-world geography, cities and landscapes.

02

Move

Explore the design of connected transport experiences across land, air and water.

03

Live

Imagine everyday activities, places, services and opportunities within a persistent world.

04

Connect

Investigate how people, systems and locations could interact in a shared experience.

03 / TARGET PLATFORMS

Designed with
reach in mind.

The current long-term target is PC, consoles and mobile. Platform support, performance targets and technical feasibility will be evaluated during development. No release date or final platform feature set has been announced.
